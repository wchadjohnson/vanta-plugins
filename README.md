# Fold for ChatGPT and Claude

Fold turns photos of your clothes into ready-to-post resale listings. This plugin lets your AI
assistant take the listings you've greenlit in Fold and put them on Depop and Vinted as drafts for
you, then keep Fold up to date when something sells.

Your assistant only ever works with listings you've greenlit, and it saves **drafts** — nothing goes
live on a marketplace without you.

## Install in ChatGPT

1. In the ChatGPT app, open **Customize** → **Plugins** → **Add** → **Add a marketplace**.
2. For **Source**, enter `wchadjohnson/vanta-plugins` and choose **Add marketplace**.
3. Open the **Personal** tab → **Vanta Plugins** → **Fold**, and install it.
4. When asked, sign in to your Fold account and approve access.

Fold's plugin page has one-tap prompts to try first.

## Install in Claude

1. Open the Claude app → **Customize** → **Plugins** → **Add**, and add the marketplace
   `wchadjohnson/vanta-plugins`.
2. Install **Fold**, then sign in to your Fold account when asked.

## Try it

- "Draft my ready Fold listings on Depop."
- "Put my ready Fold listings on Vinted as drafts."
- "My jacket sold on Depop — mark it sold in Fold."

## Stay in control

- See which assistants are connected, and disconnect any of them instantly, in Fold under
  **Account → AI assistant**.
- Your assistant signs in with your Fold account. You never paste a password or key into chat.
